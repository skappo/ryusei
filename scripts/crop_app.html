Interactive Guide

This utility formats your custom image datasets to the exact 224x224 square layout required natively by Teachable Machine.

Interface Controls

⚠️ Resolution Guard Any loaded image smaller than 224x224 is automatically rejected, deleted from queue, and skipped.
⚡ Auto-Batch Click the green button to instantly center-crop and resize the entire folder at macro speeds!
Left Click Crop manually selected region and save. Behavior shifts based on toggles.
Right Click Finalize current file and advance queue manually. Marks Green ✓ or Gray ✕.
Scroll Wheel Zooms smoothly. Minimum scale lock active to prevent out of bound crops.

Pro Tips

• **Resolution Gatekeeper:** Images that do not meet the base dimensional limit ($< 224\text{px}$) will turn Red ✕ in the sidebar list.

• Disable your browser's download prompt via: Settings -> Downloads -> Turn off "Ask where to save each file".
